# assembly and acceptance

this is a planning checklist, not a replacement for product-specific instructions. exact assembly steps remain dependent on the contributed hardware.

1. measure the available floor area, chair footprint through its range, entry/exit clearance, and existing equipment.
2. map the intended chair, eye, screen, tray, and mounting positions. verify arm reach and minimum height before selecting products.
3. check manufacturer documentation for every load, mount, surface, orientation, and retention mechanism. include adapters and trays in the mass. resolve unknown load-bearing combinations before assembly.
4. assemble according to manufacturer instructions. check stability and fasteners before adding devices. keep cables clear of moving parts and preserve ventilation.
5. verify that devices cannot slide out, mounts do not drift, the structure does not tip, and the user can enter and exit freely. do not position an unverified suspended device above a person.
6. try a short session, record comfort and reach observations, and adjust. document limitations, not just a successful photograph.
7. add a build report with exact components and test conditions. field experience does not establish clinical benefit or certification.
