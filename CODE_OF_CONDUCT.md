# community standards

be welcoming across countries, languages, abilities, ages, backgrounds, and budgets. critique a claim or design without attacking a person. harassment, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and undisclosed promotion are not welcome.

respect privacy and consent. do not pressure anyone to disclose health information, buy equipment, or try a protocol. describe personal experience as personal experience.

maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close a discussion, or restrict repeated abuse. use github's report-abuse controls for sensitive incidents; do not publish private evidence in an issue. for ordinary moderation concerns, ask a maintainer in the relevant thread without disclosing personal information.
